# Privacy Policy for DayOff (Ρεπό)

**Last updated:** January 30, 2026

## Overview

DayOff ("Ρεπό") is a schedule tracking application designed for delivery professionals. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect any personal data.**

All information you enter in the app (work schedule, shift preferences, settings) is stored **locally on your device only**. We do not:

- Collect personal information
- Track your location
- Access your contacts
- Send data to external servers
- Use analytics or tracking services
- Display third-party advertisements

## Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

- Work schedule configuration
- Day off preferences
- Shift type (morning/evening)
- App settings (language, theme, font size)
- Notification preferences

This data is stored using Android's DataStore and Room database, which are secure local storage mechanisms.

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Notifications | To remind you about your upcoming shifts |
| Exact Alarm | To schedule precise notification times |
| Boot Completed | To reschedule notifications after device restart |

These permissions are used solely for the notification feature and do not involve any data transmission.

## Data Sharing

**We do not share any data with third parties** because we do not collect any data in the first place.

## Children's Privacy

This app does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.
